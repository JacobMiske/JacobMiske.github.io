I'm a mechanical engineer working where robotics, 3D printing and materials meet. Right now that means
shape-changing robots and printed foams for my PhD at Northeastern, plus building a foam company.
Before that I spent seven years running a research reactor at MIT.

A full resume is on [jacobmiske.com](https://www.jacobmiske.com/p/blog-page_23.html), and publications are on
the [Research & Projects](/research/) page.

*Jun 2026 – present · Boston, MA*

Started the business; building the technology and selling the product.

### PhD Candidate — Transformative Robotics Lab, Northeastern University
*Sep 2023 – present · Boston, MA*

Developing shape-changing surface robots and 3D printed foams. I was
[featured by the College of Engineering](https://coe.northeastern.edu/news/creating-change-through-innovation/)

### Print Process Intern — Formlabs
*Apr 2026 – Aug 2026 · Boston, MA*

Through experiments and work with several teams, I changed an important but previously overlooked component on two
3D printers. The change is reducing the total print failure rate for some customers by more than 1%, which saves
a large amount of material at scale.

*May 2024 – Aug 2024 · Oak Ridge, TN*

### Senior Engineer — Physical Sciences Inc.
