## Research projects

- **Shape-changing, auxetic robotics:** reconfigurable auxetic devices for robotic surface manipulation
  ([RADs paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00072))
- **Printed stochastic foams:** viscous thread printing of soft robotic fingers and bodies
  ([RoboSoft 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/RoboSoft63089.2025.11020838))
- **Anisotropic and chiral foams** and **ML (RNN) modeling of mechanical lattices**
- **3D printed heat exchangers**, including metal printing work at Formlabs
- **Atmospheric water extraction:** man-portable systems built around moisture-absorbing foams
- **RF and high-voltage hardware:** high-energy microwave transmitters, ultra-lightweight wideband RF hardware,
  custom solenoids and non-linear transmission lines, and
  ±10 kV power supplies with a PyQt5 control interface
- **Radiation measurement:** ham radio measurements of background radiation, and a campus-wide background radiation
  survey at MIT
- **Other work:** high-torque electrostatic clutching, indium-tin-oxide heat absorbers, multispectral imaging
  equipment, and an FPV drone attachment kit

### Course capstones at MIT

- **Variable-temperature cryocooler (2.013):** a cryocooler design with a wide operating temperature range for
  storing many kinds of biological samples, shaped by conversations with biologists and pathologists.
- **Li-6 neutron detector (22.033):** a [low-cost neutron detector](http://web.mit.edu/nse/news/2019/nse-design-challenge.html)
  for agricultural moisture sensing, built by a team of four and presented in Singapore.
