Jacob Miske

I'm a mechanical engineer and PhD candidate in the Transformative Robotics Lab at Northeastern University. I work on shape-changing surface robots and 3D printed foams. I'm also CTO of InLoop Foam, Inc.

Before Northeastern I studied mechanical engineering and nuclear science at MIT, and spent seven years as a licensed operator at the MIT Nuclear Reactor Laboratory. Since then I've worked at Physical Sciences Inc., Oak Ridge National Laboratory (as a GRO Scholar) and Formlabs.
